# Independent newcomer acceptance script

This is a proposed human test, not evidence that external users were tested.
Use the local static preview on desktop and a phone/real mobile browser. Avoid
coaching until the person has answered. Record their words, confusion and device;
no participant identity or recording is required.

1. Open the first screen. Ask: “What decision is this project helping with?”
   Pass: distinguishes a likely visitor from someone whose visit advertising might
   change; identifies the frozen no-targeting recommendation.
2. Follow the OFF/ON guide for “Visits anyway”, then “Could be persuaded”.
   Pass: explains why the first case does not add a visit; recognizes these as
   invented illustrations, not observed personal causal labels.
3. Open The study. Ask why the ad-assigned group exists if the policy says zero ads.
   Pass: separates historical assignment, hypothetical policy and actual deployment;
   describes the observed-rate gap without saying it proves advertising caused it.
4. Open The evidence; select 30% reach and a different ranking. Read the example.
   Pass: says 300 assignments out of 1,000 eligible; additional-visit estimate uses
   all 1,000 as denominator, not 300. Does not call the highest dot the best policy.
5. Expand assumptions and exact values; change only to another saved propensity.
   Pass: finds the scenario/interval labels, recognizes assumptions and simultaneous
   versus pointwise uncertainty; understands an interval crossing zero is not
   equivalence. Ask what happens to the 765 family when fewer points are shown.
6. Open Decision Lab. Confirm default no targeting; explore an alternative and
   a cost that makes it infeasible. Pass: distinguishes hypothetical finances
   from validated ROI; sees no automatic affordable-policy trimming or new selection.
7. Open The decision and the technical disclosures. Ask how positive diagnostics
   can coexist with abstention. Pass: describes the frozen conservative development
   rule and says neither ineffectiveness nor universal optimality is established.
8. Find creator, code, case study, model card and licenses. Navigate by keyboard,
   use the skip link, inspect visible focus versus selected states, scroll the exact
   table horizontally and reload a chapter link. Pass: no trapped/hidden controls,
   headings behind the header, unreadable chart labels or loss of context.

Record PASS / confusion / blocker for each task. Correctness of a rehearsed answer
is not evidence of scientific validity. Any scientific misconception or inaccessible
control blocks publication pending a focused correction and recheck; do not change
frozen results to improve the story. Record real tests only after they happen.
